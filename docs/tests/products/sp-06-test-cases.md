AD previews current stock/held carts/draft receipts, confirms discontinuation or unused-product deletion, separately acknowledges positive stock for discontinuation, and can reactivate. Referenced/positive-stock products cannot be physically deleted; preserve financial snapshots, quantity and cost. Discontinued products leave the POS/current active-product selector.

SAN_PHAM_GIA_DANH_MUC explicitly covers SP-06 in docs/api/auth-giai-thich.md. One indexed preview query counts held/draft references and checks seven external reference families. A read-committed transaction locks stock then product, validates current version/confirmations and applies atomic status/version or deletes only the never-referenced zero-stock product's owned price/stock configuration. FK rejection is retained as a final guard. Category hidden state is preserved; reactivation does not silently unhide it. Successful actor/operation IDs are logged without payloads.
